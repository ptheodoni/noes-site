George Berkeley

Three Dialogues between Hylas and Philonous

in Opposition to Sceptics and Atheists

Two friends walk in a garden on three mornings and argue whether matter exists outside the mind. Hylas defends matter; Philonous, “the friend of mind”, speaks for Berkeley. The English text of the third edition (1734) and a new Greek translation in facing columns, with an introduction, section titles and a glossary (in Greek).

Christine de Pizan

The Book of the City of Ladies

Le Livre de la Cité des Dames (1405) · Το Βιβλίο της Πόλης των Κυριών

Series “Women Philosophers”

One of the first works of European literature written by a woman in defence of women against the misogyny of learned men. The narrator is visited by three ladies, Reason, Rectitude and Justice, who charge her with building a city for the worthy women of all ages. The Middle French text and a new Greek translation in facing columns.
