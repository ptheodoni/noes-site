George Berkeley

Three Dialogues between Hylas and Philonous

in Opposition to Sceptics and Atheists

Greek translation & editing: Panagiota Theodoni

Two friends walk in a garden on three mornings and argue whether matter exists outside the mind. Hylas defends matter; Philonous, “the friend of mind”, speaks for Berkeley. The English text of the third edition (1734) and a new Greek translation in facing columns, with an introduction, section titles and a glossary (in Greek).
