Voice · Critical Thought · Emancipation

FemMe Radio

Philosophy and social critique begin with the deconstruction of established hierarchies. FemMe Radio is an independent feminist radio station devoted to socio-political thought, the deconstruction of patriarchy and women’s emancipation.

Through shows, dialogue and research, we give a platform to the historically silenced voices of women in philosophy and science, connecting today’s social reality with radical thought.

Language · Cognition · Authentic Texts

Logoplex

The mind thinks through the limits and possibilities of language. Logoplex is an interactive environment for exploring and learning languages that combines the cognitive challenge of a game (Scrabble-style) with immersion in authentic texts.

From ancient and folk tradition to poetry, history, literature and philosophy, language is approached not merely as a grammatical code, but as a living carrier of culture, memory and mental formation.

🔬 Logoplex is also a research project of the Theodoni Lab →
