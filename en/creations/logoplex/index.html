Logoplex

Language, Cognition & Authentic Texts

The mind thinks through the limits and possibilities of language. Logoplex is an interactive environment for exploring and learning languages that combines the cognitive challenge of a game (Scrabble-style) with immersion in authentic texts.

From ancient and folk tradition to poetry, history, literature and philosophy, language is approached not merely as a grammatical code, but as a living carrier of culture, memory and mental formation.

🔬 Logoplex is also a research project: Linguistic Computation & LLM Limitations, at the Theodoni Lab →
