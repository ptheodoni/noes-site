About

Our Vision

Noes is a scientific and philosophical initiative that aims to close the gap between academic research and society.

We believe science and philosophy should not stay locked inside universities. Our aim is to give people the right tools to approach them at their real depth — without oversimplifying.

Our Pillars

Publications

Writing and publishing scientific and philosophical works.

Seminars

Interactive, in-person seminars.

Research

An open intellectual gymnasium: research for everyone, academic mentoring, grant partnerships and lab hosting, such as the Theodoni Lab. Within it: we help students (BSc, MSc, PhD) complete their theses, guide independent researchers in designing and publishing their ideas, and offer hands-on training in modern research tools.

Consulting

Cognitive neuroscience for individuals and companies: private sessions, training, scientific support.

Creations

At Noes we build platforms for education and for social and civic engagement: tools that bring knowledge and dialogue closer to everyone.

FemMe Radio

Independent, non-profit online radio: around the clock, talk, music and dialogue on women’s emancipation and the dismantling of patriarchy.

Logoplex

Language learning through play and authentic texts: Chinese, Arabic, Modern and Ancient Greek and more. All languages at logoplex.app.

Who we are

Noes is a team of active researchers, educators and creators from the sciences, philosophy and the humanities. We share the conviction that knowledge belongs to everyone: each of us brings our own research into our seminars, labs and creations.

The team is open. You can give your own seminar or start your own lab at Noes.

Legal form. Noes currently operates as an independent initiative; its members take part in research programmes as independent or affiliated researchers through partner institutions. As the core team takes shape, we plan to establish a legal entity (a non-profit civil company or social cooperative), so that Noes itself can act as host institution, submitting and managing research grants.

The Founder

Noes was founded by Dr. Panagiota Theodoni, Principal Investigator of the Theodoni Lab.

She has a background in Physics and Theoretical & Computational Neuroscience, and postgraduate studies in Philosophy. She has worked at the University of Athens, NCSR “Demokritos”, Panteion University, the Open University of Cyprus, Pompeu Fabra University, the Centre de Recerca Matemàtica, NYU Shanghai, ECNU Shanghai and NYU (Center for Neural Science and Department of Philosophy).

After supervising students at the University of Athens and NYU, she decided to create an independent space for person-centred research mentorship.

Political stance

Our philosophy: Against the myth of political neutrality

Noes, a centre for education and research, was born in 2026 into a world of deep crises and upheaval: a world where patriarchy remains deeply rooted, fascism is dangerously resurging and new forms of colonialism are intensifying. From the genocide in Palestine to the wars in Sudan and in every corner of the Earth, these crises expose the dead ends of contemporary capitalism and of unchecked technological power.

At the same time, within the dominant academic and institutional structures of power, free critical thought and political expression are being stifled: researchers, educators and creators who resist are often isolated, silenced and erased.

In the face of this, we refuse the hypocritical cloak of neutrality. Thought, knowledge and research are deeply political acts. At Noes we create an independent, safe space for expression, encounter and collective action.

We consciously stand:

  • With the oppressed and with all who struggle for dignity, self-determination and social justice.
  • Against fascism, Zionism, imperialism and every form of colonialism.
  • In active solidarity with the just struggle of the Palestinian people for freedom, and with every people fighting against subjugation. We support the BDS movement.

For us, research, language, education and creativity are not mechanisms that reproduce power, but tools of emancipation and resistance.
