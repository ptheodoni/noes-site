01

Tailored research & active participation

Research for everyone

Whether you are an independent thinker outside academia with a burning question or someone eager to experience how research is genuinely conducted, Noes provides the space, framework and scientific mentorship to explore it.

  • Explore your own question. Bring your idea, hypothesis or conceptual puzzle. We work together one-on-one to frame it as a rigorous research problem, map the scientific and philosophical literature, design the methodology and develop your project step by step.
  • Join an ongoing lab project. Participate directly in active research within our labs (computational neuroscience, decision-making, philosophy of mind). Learn how to formulate models, analyse data and engage in dialectical peer review by contributing to real investigations.

How research is funded

The Noes Research Pool

We believe in making research sustainable and self-reinforcing. 20% of all proceeds from our personalised research mentorship and programmes is channelled directly into the Noes Research Pool. Whenever the pool reaches its target, an open competitive call for paid research fellowships is launched for students and early-career independent researchers.

For students and early-career researchers

Academic supervision & mentoring

Dedicated mentorship through demanding academic milestones:

  • Theses & dissertations. Methodological and theoretical supervision for BSc, MSc and PhD projects.
  • Publishing & peer review. Structuring, writing and refining papers for submission to international journals.
  • Modern research tools. Hands-on training in computational modelling, formal reasoning and experimental design.

03

Co-creating research proposals

Grant partnerships

We partner with university labs, independent researchers, research institutions and organisations to jointly develop, write and submit research proposals for international and national grants (e.g. Horizon Europe, philanthropic foundations).

  • Collaborative frameworks. Interdisciplinary proposals at the intersection of computational neuroscience, epistemic agency, formal systems and philosophy of mind.
  • Affiliated researchers. Researchers can collaborate with Noes as an intellectual home and, once Noes is a legal entity, as an institutional co-applicant.
