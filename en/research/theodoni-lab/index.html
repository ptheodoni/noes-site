Theodoni Lab

Theoretical and computational neuroscience in dialogue with philosophy of mind.

What we study

How does the brain function, perceive, think, and lead to observable behaviors? We combine theory, physics, mathematics, models, and computational simulations with philosophical questions about the mind, science, and epistemology.

Active project · Language & AI

Linguistic Computation & LLM Limitations
