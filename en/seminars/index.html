Seminars

Science & Philosophy for Everyone

These seminars were born from the conviction that scientific and philosophical research should neither stay locked within the narrow limits of academia nor be popularised in a way that loses its depth. The goal is not to “simplify” knowledge by diminishing it, but to offer the right tools so that the public can approach and understand science and philosophy at their real level.

No prior specialised knowledge is required — only curiosity and a willingness to engage in dialogue.

Modelled on the Science as a Hobby initiative, we create an interactive space where deep knowledge becomes accessible, alive and part of everyday life.
